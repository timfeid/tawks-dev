tawks sandbox

A hosted stand-in for tawks, for trying games before they go into a niche. Nothing here touches real tawks: lobbies live in memory and close after half an hour without use.

Play the games connected to the sandbox, or make a sandbox bot to connect your own. Then run your bot with:

RSPC_URL=https:///rspc
EVENTS_WS_URL=wss:///events
BOT_TOKEN=<your sandbox token>

The Game activities docs explain the rest.
