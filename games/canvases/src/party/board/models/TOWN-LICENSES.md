# Frostline City assets

- `traffic-{sedan,taxi,suv,delivery}.glb`: original Kenney **Car Kit 3.1**, https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit . CC0 1.0; original archive license in `TRAFFIC-CAR-LICENSE.txt`.
- `town-building-{a..n,skyscraper-a..e}.glb`: original Kenney **City Kit (Commercial) 2.1**, https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial . CC0 1.0; original archive license in `TOWN-BUILDINGS-LICENSE.txt`.
- Existing snowy trees, street lamps, stalls and fountain retain their original Kenney licenses in `README.md`.

Original archives were downloaded from the creator's normal public download links. The original embedded palettes are included in every GLB, so there are no external texture requests. Cars normalize to three world units long at runtime. `traffic-rental.glb` is the same Car Kit’s open-cockpit `race.glb`. Traffic lanes/collision and pedestrian signals are server authoritative; road markings and signals are generated geometry.

- `objective-candy.glb`: Kenney Food Kit, CC0, original candy-bar-wrapper.glb; https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit . Embedded original texture, unmodified model. License: CANDY-LICENSE.txt.

- `candy-van.glb`: Kenney Car Kit `van.glb`, original body faces assigned an off-white PBR material; the original palette texture remains embedded for glass, lights and tyres. Same CC0 license above.
