# Party sounds

Gameplay effects currently use the synthesized fallbacks in `sfx.ts`. Optional
recordings named `<sound>-<n>.ogg` can be supplied at this level; none of those
optional packs are bundled here.

## Frostline city ambience

The four bundled recordings in `city/` are **CC0 1.0** (public domain), free
for redistribution and commercial use. `city/CREDITS.json` records the author,
primary source, original download, processing and SHA-256 of each shipped file.
The city uses street ambience, indoor crowd walla, a shop refrigerator hum and
an occasional distant police siren, plus synthesized wind, engines and footsteps.
The ambience slider is independent of gameplay effects. All voices stop when
muted, suspended, leaving the city or disposing the canvas.

These are local bundled assets; gameplay does not contact the source sites.
