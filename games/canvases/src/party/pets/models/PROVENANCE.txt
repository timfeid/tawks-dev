Modifications
- Texture repainted into flat colour regions matching each pet's catalog body
  and accent colours (rules in recolour.json), shrunk from 512 to 256 px and
  embedded in the GLB. Eyes, mouths and teeth keep the original layout.
  Ember: orange body, gold belly and horns, deep orange wings.
  Puddle: blue body, pale blue belly, gold beak and feet.
  Sprout: green body, pink flower, cream spines.
  Fizz: gold and dark stripes, pale wings.
  Mochi: pink body, deeper pink ears and snout, cream teeth.
- Materials replaced by one plain PBR material per pet; the app draws it
  with toon shading.
- Animations trimmed and renamed: Idle (the pack's Idle, or Flying for the
  dragon and bee), Cheer (Dance, or Bite_Front for flyers) and Hop (Jump,
  where present). All other clips and non-character objects are dropped.
- Geometry, rig and skin weights are unchanged (840 to 1,400 vertices, under
  1,600 triangles, 5 to 17 bones). Each GLB is 115 to 160 KB.

Reproduce
build-pets.py and recolour.json in this folder rebuild all five GLBs and the coats from the
archive with Blender 4.2 (the bpy module); the command is in the script header.
Copies of the GLBs live in apps/tawkathon-native/assets/pets for the native game.

Coats
The pet store's coats (Midnight, Frost and Candy) are the same source
textures repainted by build-pets.py with the colours each pet's "coats" entry
in recolour.json swaps into its rules, so the regions and shading match the
pet's own texture. They are written as coats/<pet>-<coat>.png, 256 px, and
drawn on the unchanged GLBs in place of the embedded texture. Same CC0 source
and license as above.

Pet gear
The pet hats and neckwear are not from the pack: they are original
primitives (spheres, cones, rings and boxes) listed in ../gear.json and built
in code by both clients, provided under CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No trademarked designs.
