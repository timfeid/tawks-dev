You are nothing until you become something.

At night, choose one player to observe. You become the role you see, and immediately perform its action, if it has one.

What happens next depends on the role:

  • The Unmarked, The Hunter, The Scorned: You take that role and do nothing else.
  • The Bound, The Hollow: You join their team and wake with them when they are called.
  • The Witness, The Tangle, The Drunk, The Robber: You perform that role’s action immediately, but do not wake again when it’s called later.
  • The Follower: You join the Hollow’s team.
  • The Sleepless: After The Sleepless closes her eyes, you wake to see if you are still The Echo.

You are a reflection — and what you reflect may not reflect you.
